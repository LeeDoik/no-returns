## Direction and scope

Prioritize shape, materials and motion over cost savings as requested. Preserve cooperative rules, map routes, collision dimensions, English source copy and complete Korean counterparts. Depict a tired postal facility in exaggerated stylized 3D. Muted sage facilities form the background; ochre/cream workers and parcels carry focus; lime and dusty pink signal interactions. Model rounded edges and handles in geometry and distinguish paper, rubber, painted metal and timber. Do not indiscriminately apply one texture to floors and walls.

Use Higgsfield for style reference images, actual materials and character mesh generation. Clean dimensions, axes, materials, meshes and animation in Blender and export GLB. Check readability, collision agreement, shadows and online behavior in the game. Preserve `art/sneezer` as source history; place new work in `art/release-01` and runtime files in `assets/art/release-01`.

## Asset list

- Worker: distinct head, cap, workwear, gloves and boots, with locomotion and carrying motion.
- Four living parcels: cardboard, tape, lids, expressions and type-specific silhouettes; existing sneeze, jump and adhesion reactions.
- Packrat: readable face, ears, tail and motion, with appearance tied to its nest.
- Facility: reception counter, workbench, conveyor, dispatch equipment, shelving, walls, floors and lighting.
- Reactive props: cohesive cushion, spring plate, carton tower and paperwork materials with mechanical details.
- Deliverables: Blender sources, GLBs/textures, provenance/work records, actual game captures, bilingual guide and Windows build.

## Acceptance

A generated output alone is not release quality. Integrate it and inspect distance readability, animation deformation, visual/collision agreement, material orientation/seams, missing files, launch and network regressions. Final release judgment still needs external play, low-end PC performance and Steam review. Record generation IDs and sources without account credentials. Purchases and subscription changes are outside this work.
