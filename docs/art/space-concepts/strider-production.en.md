## Form

Retain a heavy high-front torso, low hanging wedge head, 2 thick front legs and 2 hind legs, and broad flat feet. Do not add a tail, extra arms, external eyes or glowing weakpoints. Use broad brown, charcoal and ochre planes for PSX material treatment. Consolidate small rocky facets into surface detail rather than separate pieces.

The head must be hidden behind the torso from the rear. Corrected an initial generated rear view that repeated the front. Generated proportions across views are not a fully consistent construction drawing and require a grey model check.

## Rig and motion proposal

Consider a generic quadruped rig with torso/spine, neck/head and four leg chains under ROOT. Joint dots are conceptual, not a final hierarchy or count. Consolidate toes without independent articulation for the first version. Define neck/head ranges that avoid floor and foreleg intersections.

| Motion | Production criterion |
|---|---|
| IDLE | Four planted feet with small breathing motion |
| WALK | Short slow alternating steps; check sliding and contact |
| TURN | Step through turns instead of twisting planted feet |
| WARN | Lift head and torso to telegraph attack |
| ATTACK | Test a short forefoot strike/weight shift supported by remaining feet |

Attack motion is proposed; damage, range, timing, detection and pursuit rules need separate decisions. Retain the unkillable outer threat entering after suppression shutdown. Scale, polygon/texture budgets and joint count remain undecided.
