The user confirmed 0.2 works and asked to continue. Implement the next cargo from the existing concept: Clinger. Use subagent-driven-development for the independent attachment controller, with root integration and requesting-code-review before completion.

**Goal:** Three cargo types coexist; Clinger sticks to one worker or non-Clinger cargo, and Sneezer breaks that attachment.

**Architecture:** `clinger.gd` is a host-driven attachment controller with visual presentation and replicated state. Cargo retains physics/ownership/delivery; main updates attachment after cargo motion and before publishing. Compact cargo wire arrays keep the three-cargo snapshots within the existing 1280-byte budget. No transport replacement.

**Stack:** Existing Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, ENet and primitive geometry. Korean default UI; English source copy and complete paired documents. No Steam integration, final assets, Hopper or four-player expansion.

## Decisions

- ID 3 Clinger starts at `(2, 0.55, 4)`, destination A. Standard and Sneezer remain IDs 1 and 2. Quota remains 5 in 180 seconds.
- A free Clinger within 0.95m of a cargo center or worker chest can attach if solid geometry does not intervene. Pick the nearest eligible target. Exclude itself, hidden/recovering/inactive cargo and Clingers. No chains.
- It remains attached for 5 seconds. A worker moves at 70% normal speed but keeps control and may carry one ordinary held slot. An attached Clinger cannot be picked up directly. Carry its target cargo to move both.
- Store the world-space attachment offset at contact. Sweep the Clinger box against solid geometry while following; break the bond if obstructed or separated by more than 2m. Do not drag cargo through walls or across worker respawns.
- Timeout, Sneezer impact, target loss, return, shift end or restart releases the bond. A 2-second cooldown prevents immediate reattachment; ordinary E pickup remains possible during cooldown. Starting/recovering Clinger has 1 second of grace.
- If its cargo target is successfully dispatched, award the Clinger once and recover it too. Wrong-bay/lost target recovery detaches without awarding Clinger points. Normal independent dispatch still works.
- Render a lime crate with raised sticky pads, a visible connection and localized attached/cooldown countdown. Sound is optional; silent play must remain clear.
- Host decides all bonds and breaks. Guests receive target kind/ID/time, cargo position and worker speed state. Replicated rendering does not acquire targets or apply pushes.
