## 2. Pitch

NO RETURNS is a cooperative delivery game where the packages are alive. Sneezing boxes, sticky cargo, and questionable teamwork turn a simple shipping job into a series of recoverable disasters. Learn each package's behavior and use it to help your crew finish the shift.

**One-line hook:** The packages are alive. Ship them anyway.

## 5. Coverage policy to finalize before invitations

Proposed policy: allow creators to record, stream, and monetize ordinary gameplay footage, with no requirement for positive coverage. Confirm the policy with the publishing owner and ensure included music, artwork, and third-party material permit the intended use before publishing it.

For each test, state whether public coverage is allowed, whether there is an embargo, and which build limitations should be disclosed. Do not promise immunity from third-party copyright claims. Keep a practical contact for resolving rights questions.

## 6. Feedback form

Collect only information needed for the test. Tell participants if a session or feedback will be recorded.

1. How many people played, and which build did you use?
2. Could everyone join? If not, what happened and what message appeared?
3. What did you think the shared goal was?
4. Describe the most memorable package interaction.
5. Was any failure hard to understand? What did you expect instead?
6. Did a mistake leave anyone unable to help for too long?
7. Did you discover a useful trick with another player?
8. Did you want another shift? Why or why not?
9. Were the camera, controls, text, or warnings difficult to read?
10. For performance/network issues only: what Windows version, hardware, general region, and connection conditions were involved?
