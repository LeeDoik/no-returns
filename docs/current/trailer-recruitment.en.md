## Format selection

1. **Delivery-company recruitment advertisement — recommended.** Quickly communicates survival logistics, employees, cooperative delivery and corporate irresponsibility. Alternate the advertised world with field conditions.
2. New-employee safety film. Makes behavioral rules easy to explain, but instruction can overwhelm atmosphere.
3. Recovered incident recording. Delivers direct horror but leaves less room for necessities, residents and corporate hypocrisy.

75 seconds is a first-edit proposal. The optimistic recruitment advertisement keeps its reassuring tone to the end while the images increasingly contradict it. Do not explain the entire history or reveal Earth's destruction mechanism. Details in [WORLD-01](world-setting.en.md) and the following copy remain proposals; this trailer request does not approve every detail. Use placeholders for company branding if no approved name/logo exists rather than silently finalizing a new brand.

## Image and sound direction

- Output 16:9; frame vintage company footage inside a 4:3 area. Field first-person footage uses the wider frame. Preserve the game's PSX low resolution, rough surfaces and dark lighting.
- Company footage uses steady compositions and restrained color. Field footage uses uneasy stillness, brief movement and practical equipment lights. Do not apply shake and glitches indiscriminately to every shot.
- Keep a friendly, practiced corporate-announcer performance. Do not turn the voice into a villain at the end; the world and images create suspicion.
- Begin with a short, upbeat company theme. Lower it during field shots to expose suppression machinery, parcel friction, footsteps and the printer. Recontextualize the final confirmation tone as frightening.
- Conceal the full creature and mystery answer, but clearly show employees, parcels and cooperative actions. Avoid suggesting an atmosphere-only solo horror game.
- English voice-over is the source, with separate Korean and English subtitles. Tiny disclaimer text is a secondary joke, not a carrier of essential information.

## Required material and practical production order

Required groups: company archive/route graphics; ship departure; CINDER DEPOT carrying, suppression, distraction and extraction; receiving terminal/receipt; title; narration, music and effects. The entire map need not reach final-art quality, but **visible spaces and actual interactions** must reach the intended presentation quality.

The new Cinder blockout does not connect enemy pursuit, networking or delivery settlement; do not assume it can immediately record cooperative deliveries. Connect those features or arrange verified features in a filming space before capturing actual gameplay.

Production order:
