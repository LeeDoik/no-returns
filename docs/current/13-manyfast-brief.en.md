Manyfast project: `427d2942-d820-4899-abbf-664df084d5ed`

## Subject

A stream-oriented cooperative game where friends turn random situations into stories of mishaps and recovery — exploratory concept brief.

## Background and status

Revision 1, 2026-09-11. The user permits a concept completely different from the existing delivery game and wants entertaining random situations plus hands-on analysis of controls, UI and systems. Explore cooperation first; combat and competitive elements remain undecided. Replacing the existing game has not been decided. This is a proposal rather than an implementation specification. Each field contains complete Korean and English counterparts.

## Goal

Aim for objectives and accident causes that newcomers can understand, responses discussed with teammates, and a desire to retry with different choices. Player responses to situations should drive enjoyment more than arbitrary outcomes. Validate enjoyment among friends without viewers first.

## Problem hypotheses

Design hypotheses: long explanations, unreadable status information, unpredictable control failures and long elimination waits interrupt shared play. Random events that determine outcomes without response opportunities may feel unfair. These are not conclusions verified through user research or human play.

## Candidates

Candidate A: Defective Heroes — select abilities with benefits and drawbacks for cooperative combat. Explore healing knockback as both a falling hazard and a way to cross gaps.
Candidate B: Mech Driving Test — divide movement, arms, weapons and power on one robot while handling faults and tests. Validate sustained activity for every role.
Candidate C: Wish Carefully — shared wishes grant benefits and costs that change how the next space is solved. Explore alternative uses for problems such as an oversized weapon jamming in doors.
A is the suggested starting point; the user has not selected it. Decide the core concept, camera, devices, maximum players, session length, revival, rewards and voice rules after selection.

## Differentiation hypothesis

The differentiation hypothesis is that the same variable can cause an accident and enable a cooperative solution. Support learned responses over repeated play rather than relying only on one-off jokes or intentional friendly interference. No superiority or genre novelty claim is established.

## Audience

Streamers playing and talking with friends, and regular cooperative-game players enjoying the same situations. Viewers are an observation audience; direct participation and voting integrations are not required decisions. Supported player counts and platforms remain open.

## Flow and reference study

Proposed flow: invite friends → learn objectives and basic controls → perform a shared task → encounter random variation → choose a response → accident or success → rescue and recover → review results → retry.
Suggested hands-on study: PEAK for movement and rescue, R.E.P.O. for object handling, and Overcooked! 2 for UI and division of labor. If combat is selected, study class tools in Deep Rock Galactic. Compare Human Fall Flat for physics controls and Lethal Company for asymmetric information and extraction decisions. Hands-on play has not been performed.
Official references: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/ ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/ ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/728880/ ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/548430/ ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/477160/ ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/

## Validation plan

Validation plan: record time to understand objectives, unintended control outcomes, missed UI information, moments genuinely requiring a teammate, alternative solutions to the same variation, post-death re-entry delays and voluntary replay intent. Check whether accident causes remain understandable in muted footage. Targets, pass thresholds and sample size are undecided; no measurements exist.

## Risks and next decision

Risks: unsolvable random combinations, drawbacks becoming exhausting friendly interference, unequal role enjoyment, physics and networking costs, and controls amusing viewers while frustrating players. Validate a minimal scene before expanding combinations. The next decision is A/B/C concept selection; do not finalize feature lists or numeric rules beforehand. Development, release and fun validation remain incomplete.
