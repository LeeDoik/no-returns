## Produced deliverables

The user requested a Higgsfield trailer draft referencing existing concepts. Dialogue and overall structure follow the [75-second recruitment-ad treatment](trailer-recruitment.en.md). Output is 1280×720, 24fps, 75 seconds, with temporary English narration, burned-in Korean captions and matching Korean/English SRTs.

Higgsfield Kling 3.0 generated two six-second shots: delivery movement and an outer-creature approach. Remaining material consists of crops/camera moves across ten concept shots, company cards and blackout. Cuts/titles were edited in Higgsfield-hosted Higgsedit; FFmpeg combined speech/subtitles. Temporary speech uses Microsoft Zira; the motif, drone and confirmation chime were synthesized from scratch. Generated clip ambience was mixed in. Seedance was rejected by the current plan; no Seedance generation ran.

The proposed Earth-life archive is replaced with an existing exterior concept and company cards. Cooperative distraction/rescue were not filmed as continuous performed actions. The entire picture is marked as a concept, not gameplay. Older ship/terminal concepts do not prove current model implementation. No game code, scenes or models changed.
