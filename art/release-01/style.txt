Stylized handcrafted 3D with tactile painted surfaces and restrained wear, rounded substantial silhouettes, deliberate asymmetry and clearly separated mechanical parts. The postal facility uses muted sage enamel, charcoal rubber and warm aged timber; workers and parcels use ochre canvas and cream cardboard, while lime green and dusty coral identify unusual interactions. Soft warm practical lighting meets cool skylight, creating a slightly absurd working depot rather than a fantasy ruin. Readable silhouettes and material separation suit a free third-person camera; concept objects use a three-quarter isometric view.
