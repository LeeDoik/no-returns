# Sneezer — Reference-informed concept v0.2

[한국어](ps1-v02.ko.md)

Status: image proposal for review. Final design approval, 3D model, and game integration are incomplete.

The [concept](sneezer-states-ps1-v02.png) edits the [previous PS1 concept](sneezer-states-ps1-v01.png) with the built-in image generation tool. The [generation prompt](generation-ps1-v02.json) is saved alongside it.

The simple forms, coarse low-resolution materials, muted colors, and crude shading observed around 2:44, 3:39, and 5:29 of the user's [reference video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_qYYAbQpM0) informed the graphics. The video itself was not submitted to the generation tool; observations were described in the prompt. Its horror, characters, environments, and game concept were not adopted. The cooperative delivery and absurd sneezing-box concept remain intact.

Changes: removed volumetric cheeks and eyebrows in favor of a flat facial texture, simple cuboid, faded cardboard, and gray-white breath effect. The idle, warning, and release states remain.

Visual review: the face reads as largely flat and the three expressions are distinguishable. A small nose-like pixel mark and soft ground shadows remain. This is not a 3D asset with verified texture resolution or polygon count. No code, maps, or scenes belonging to the other session were modified.
